    MY CITY · HOW TO PLAY

    Goal: Be the first to hold $10,000 in cash, or become the last remaining player. Everyone begins with $1,500.

    YOUR TURN

    Roll two dice, move clockwise, and resolve the square. Buy unowned properties or Car Shops when you land on them. Pay rent to an owner when you land on their space. Passing GO earns $200.

    INVESTMENTS

    You may build on any property you own, without requiring a complete color set. Houses take 1 development slot, apartments 2 and businesses 3. Blocks 1–4 have 3, 4, 5 and 6 slots per property. Buildings increase rent; Car Shops increase in rent as you collect more of them.

    TRADING & MONEY

    Offer to buy properties or trade properties and cash with other players. Sell developments to the bank for half their cost. Once all your developments are sold, you can mortgage properties for half their purchase price. To unmortgage, repay the mortgage plus 10% interest. Mortgaged spaces collect no rent. Owning a complete original property set doubles its undeveloped base rent, even though all property tiles appear white until purchased.

    PAYMENTS & BANKRUPTCY

    When you cannot pay, raise funds by selling developments, mortgaging, or arranging trades. If you still cannot pay, declare bankruptcy. Rent debt transfers your remaining cash and eligible assets to the creditor; bank debt returns remaining spaces to the market.

    LEAVING THE GAME

    A player may resign. Their properties and Car Shops return to the market, and they leave the match. Resigning while owing a debt follows the bankruptcy rules.

    JAIL & EVENTS

    Go To Jail moves you directly to Jail without GO money. You can pay $50 or try for doubles on your next three turns; after a third failed attempt you must pay $50. Bullish pays rewards; Bearish costs money; Unexpected Expense charges based on your owned houses, apartments, and businesses. Free Parking has no effect.

    DICE SIDE BETS

    Before rolling, two active local players can agree to an equal-stake wager (up to $200 each) on whether the next dice total is higher or lower than 7. Seven is a push. Stakes are reserved until the dice settle.

    CASINO

    Roulette opens to all active players at the end of every round. Chips are $1, $5, $10, $20, $50 and $100, with a $100 total bet cap per player (or their cash balance, if lower). Single-number, color, parity, dozen, and column bets pay American roulette odds.

    Development prices, rents, and event fees are test values based on classic Monopoly's $1,500 starting economy. These may change after balancing. Local play rules are implemented here; online Supabase rooms have a separate rules implementation.

    PROPERTY MANAGEMENT

    PLAYER-TO-PLAYER OFFERS

    Make a cash offer to purchase another player's property, or offer your space in exchange. Local players accept, decline, or counter on this device.

    NEGOTIATE A DEAL

    Each side may include a property and cash. Properties must have no developments before they are traded; mortgages transfer with the deed.
